Common questions

How much does it cost to ship a car from Florida?
Price depends on the route, the size of the vehicle, whether it runs, and open versus enclosed transport. Send the pickup ZIP, delivery ZIP and the year, make and model and you get a firm price, not a teaser that goes up after booking.
How long does it take to ship a car from South Florida to New York?
Typically 3 to 5 days from pickup. Chicago runs 4 to 6 days, Texas 3 to 4 days, and California 7 to 10 days.
Can you ship a car that does not run?
Yes. Non-running vehicles are picked up on a winch-equipped truck. Tell us when you request the price so the right truck is assigned.
Is my car insured during transport?
Yes. Every carrier we assign is licensed and insured, and the car is inspected and photographed at pickup and again at delivery.
What is the difference between open and enclosed transport?
Open carriers are the standard and cost less. Enclosed carriers protect the car from weather and road debris and are used for classics, exotics and high-value vehicles.
Do you ship snowbird cars north and south?
Yes. Snowbird moves north in spring and south in fall are a large part of what we run out of South Florida. Book early in the season, when carrier space tightens.
When do I pay?
You pay the driver on delivery, after the car is inspected.
